Privacy Policy

PPT Remote: Bluetooth Clicker · com.kenny.bluetoothpresenter

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Overview

PPT Remote: Bluetooth Clicker turns a compatible Android phone into a Bluetooth presentation remote. The app works locally between your phone and a paired computer.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, user accounts, or remote servers. The app does not request Internet access.

Device permissions

The app may request Bluetooth or nearby-device permission to discover, pair with, connect to, and control a computer using Android's Bluetooth HID functionality. On supported Android versions, it may request notification permission so it can show a persistent notification while lock-screen controls are active. These permissions are used only on your device for the app's presentation-remote features.

Local preferences

Settings such as language, theme, volume-key direction, and lock-screen-control state may be stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to the developer or third parties. Removing the app clears its locally stored data according to Android's standard behavior.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant app update is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ppt@tcool.ltd.

隐私政策

PPT遥控器：蓝牙演示翻页器 · com.kenny.bluetoothpresenter

生效日期：2026年8月17日

概述

PPT遥控器通过蓝牙 HID 功能，将兼容的 Android 手机用作演示翻页器。应用仅在手机与已配对电脑之间本地工作。

数据收集与共享

本应用不收集、存储、出售或共享个人数据，不使用分析、广告 SDK、跟踪技术、用户账号或远程服务器，也不申请互联网访问权限。

设备权限

应用可能申请蓝牙或“附近的设备”权限，用于通过 Android 蓝牙 HID 功能发现、配对、连接并控制电脑。在支持的 Android 版本上，应用可能申请通知权限，以便在锁屏控制启用期间显示常驻通知。这些权限仅在您的设备上用于演示遥控功能。

本地设置

语言、主题、音量键方向和锁屏控制状态等设置可能保存在您的设备本地，不会传输给开发者或第三方。卸载应用后，这些本地数据将按 Android 的标准行为被清除。

儿童隐私

本应用并非专门面向儿童，也不会有意收集包括儿童在内的任何人的个人信息。

政策变更

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在发布相关更新前更新本政策。
